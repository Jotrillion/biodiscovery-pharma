# About

The Translational Bioinformatics and Drug Discovery Lab is dedicated to advancing therapeutic innovation through computational biology, data-driven research, and molecular discovery. Our work integrates genomics, proteomics, systems biology, and molecular modeling to identify disease mechanisms, uncover drug targets, and support the development of new treatment strategies.

Our lab focuses on using bioinformatics tools and analytical frameworks to interpret complex biological data, accelerate drug discovery, and support translational research. By combining computational analysis with biomedical knowledge, we aim to bridge the gap between basic science and clinical applications.

Through research in target identification, biomarker discovery, drug repurposing, and structure-based design, we seek to contribute to more effective, precise, and efficient therapeutic development. The lab is committed to scientific excellence, interdisciplinary collaboration, and innovation that can improve human health.
