# About

The Translational Bioinformatics and Drug Discovery Lab brings together computational biology, biomedical research, and therapeutic innovation to accelerate the discovery of new treatments. Our work focuses on analyzing biological data to uncover disease mechanisms, identify promising drug targets, and support the development of more effective therapies.

By integrating genomics, proteomics, systems biology, and molecular modeling, we translate complex biological information into actionable insights for drug development. Our research spans target identification, biomarker discovery, drug repurposing, and precision medicine, promoting a data-driven approach to improving patient outcomes.

We are committed to interdisciplinary collaboration, scientific discovery, and advancing translational research that connects computational insight with real-world medical impact. Through innovation and rigorous analysis, the lab aims to contribute to the next generation of therapeutic breakthroughs.
